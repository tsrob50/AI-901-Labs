CONTOSO IT HELPDESK GUIDE
For Contoso employees. Version 1.2, updated November 2026.
This guide is sample content for training. All names, addresses, and numbers are fictional.


1. CONTACTING THE IT HELPDESK

Helpdesk hours: Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM Central Time.
After hours, urgent issues only: call the on-call line at extension 4357 (H-E-L-P).
IT portal (open a ticket): https://itportal.contoso.com
Helpdesk email: helpdesk@contoso.com
Typical response time: 4 business hours for standard tickets, 1 hour for urgent tickets.

An urgent ticket is an issue that stops you from working completely, such as a laptop that won't start or an account that is locked during a customer meeting.


2. WI-FI NETWORKS

Contoso has two Wi-Fi networks in every office.

Staff network: CONTOSO-CORP
- For Contoso laptops and phones only.
- Sign in with your Contoso email address and password. No separate Wi-Fi password is needed.
- If your laptop won't connect, forget the CONTOSO-CORP network, restart the laptop, and connect again.

Guest network: CONTOSO-GUEST
- For visitors and personal devices.
- Connect, then accept the terms on the sign-in page that opens in the browser.
- Guest access lasts 24 hours, then you accept the terms again.
- The guest network can't reach internal sites, file shares, or printers.

Never connect a Contoso laptop to CONTOSO-GUEST. It blocks access to internal sites.


3. PASSWORDS AND SIGN-IN

Reset your own password: https://passwordreset.contoso.com
- You need your registered phone for the verification code.
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
- Passwords expire every 180 days. You get a reminder email 14 days before.

Account locked: your account locks after 10 failed sign-in attempts. It unlocks on its own after 30 minutes, or you can reset your password to unlock it right away.

Multifactor authentication (MFA): Contoso uses the Microsoft Authenticator app. If you get a new phone, open a ticket before you reset or trade in the old phone.

The IT helpdesk will never ask for your password. Never share it with anyone, including IT staff.


4. VPN (WORKING REMOTELY)

VPN app: Contoso Secure Connect (installed on every Contoso laptop).
VPN address: vpn.contoso.com
- Open Contoso Secure Connect, enter vpn.contoso.com if it isn't already filled in, and select Connect.
- Sign in with your Contoso email address and approve the request in Microsoft Authenticator.
- You only need the VPN for internal sites and file shares. Microsoft 365 apps, including Outlook, Teams, and OneDrive, work without it.
- If the VPN won't connect, check that your home internet is working, restart the laptop, and try again. If it still fails, open a ticket and include the error message.


5. LOANER LAPTOPS

Loaner laptops are available when your Contoso laptop is being repaired or replaced.
- Request a loaner on the IT portal under Hardware, then Loaner Laptop.
- Pick up loaners at the IT desk on the 2nd floor of the main office.
- You can keep a loaner laptop for up to 14 days. Ask for an extension on the original ticket if your repair takes longer.
- Loaners are wiped when they are returned, so save your files to OneDrive, not the desktop.
- Return the loaner with its charger to the IT desk.


6. NEW HARDWARE AND SOFTWARE

- Standard laptop replacement cycle: every 4 years.
- Request new software on the IT portal under Software. Approved software installs within 1 business day.
- Software not on the approved list needs manager approval and a security review, which takes about 5 business days.


7. MICROSOFT 365 SERVICE ISSUES

If Outlook, Teams, or OneDrive stop working for many people at once, it may be a Microsoft 365 service issue, not a problem with your laptop.
- Check the IT portal home page for a service alert banner.
- Microsoft also posts current service status online.
- If there is no alert and only you are affected, restart the app, then your laptop, then open a ticket.


8. WHAT THE HELPDESK CAN'T HELP WITH

The IT helpdesk does not handle expense reports, payroll, time off, or building access badges.
- Expense reports and payroll: contact Finance.
- Time off: contact HR.
- Building badges: contact Facilities.
